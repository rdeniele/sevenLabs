SevenLabs runs privately on your Windows computer.

Before you continue:

• The first launch requires an internet connection.
• Python, PyTorch, Chatterbox, Whisper, and model files are downloaded during first-time setup.
• Allow several gigabytes of free disk space and several minutes for setup.
• Voice samples, generated audio, settings, and downloaded models stay in your local user data folder.
• Only clone a voice that you own or have explicit permission to use.

After setup, previously downloaded models can be used offline.
